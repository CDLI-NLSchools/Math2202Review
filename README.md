# Math 2202 Review

A generated practice question bank for **Mathematics 2202** (Applied stream, Newfoundland and Labrador), built on the same chassis as the Math 7, 8, 9, 1201, 1202, 2200, 2201, 3200, 3201, 3202, and 3208 Review apps, with the junior scaffolding set: staged hints, worked examples, and immediate feedback.

**Live app:** https://cdli-nlschools.github.io/Math2202Review/ (once GitHub Pages is enabled)

## What it does

- Opens on a simple student face: the selected scope, one line about what will happen, and one large start button. The full builder sits collapsed under "Build your own practice". If no units are chosen, the start button opens the builder and scrolls to it.
- Draws fresh practice questions by unit or by curriculum indicator. The bank holds 194 entries covering all 85 curriculum indicators across the eight units. The six formula indicators (2.1 to 2.6) are placed by the official spreadsheet in four units: they are anchored in Unit 2 (Surface Area) and recur in Units 3, 7 and 8, where the entries are set in those units' own contexts (volume work, slope and scale work, trigonometry) rather than repeating the Unit 2 treatment.
- No mid-course or final exam presets: this course has none. Instead, each unit has a practice test modelled on the development shell's unit tests (the documented Unit 1 Test 2025 draws 10 selected-response questions at 1 mark each and 3 constructed-response questions at 3, 2 and 3 marks from variant groups of three, 18 points): 10 selected-response questions with a 2/6/2 level split, plus 3 constructed-response questions (one at 2 marks and two at 3 marks, with at least one at Level 3), for 18 marks in every unit. Practice tests are built one unit at a time; asking for one across several units returns guidance instead of a paper.
- Practice tests offer an optional soft timer modelled on a 1 hour sitting (a single setting, TEST_MINUTES, drives the label). It counts up, never locks or submits anything. Note: the development shell's own unit tests are set to 120 minutes; the 1 hour model is the project decision and is flagged as item 1 on the QA spot-check list.
- Three ways to work: practice online with check-as-you-go marking, print a practice sheet (with working space and an optional answer key), or print a unit practice test with a separate teacher key. Sheets and tests can also be downloaded as a Word file with native editable equations, or copied for Google Docs.
- Every practice question carries a staged hint (strategy, then first step), a "See a worked example" button that shows the same skill with different numbers, per-answer feedback on multiple choice questions, and a full worked solution. The worked example and the worked solution are show/hide toggles: hiding and re-showing never changes what was dealt, and a separate "Show another example" button inside the example panel is the only way to deal a fresh example (in seeded sets the sequence of examples is reproducible, press by press). The end-of-set summary lists any indicators missed and offers a "Practice what I missed" set. A collapsible reference panel sits at the top of practice sets for the units the course formula sheet covers (see below).
- Teachers can set a seed word or phrase in the builder ("Same problems for everyone"): a shared link carrying that seed gives every student the identical set, same questions, same values, same order, including the worked examples. With no seed, each student gets a fresh random set.
- Any online practice set can be downloaded for Kahoot (.xlsx, in Kahoot's template layout) or Blooket (.csv, in Blooket's template layout) to run the same multiple choice questions as a game, with the math written as readable plain text and a time limit set by question level (20, 30 or 60 seconds). The downloads live on the online practice view only; practice sheets and practice tests are show-your-work print products and do not offer them. Constructed-response questions are not exported, and the app says how many were left out.
- Contexts follow the applied stream: Math at Work workplace and everyday situations, plain short stems, both SI and imperial measures appearing as the indicators demand, and money amounts realistic for Newfoundland and Labrador (HST at 15% where tax applies, with rates stated in the stem where a question depends on them).

## Sources and originality

Items are generated from templates written for this bank. Unit placement and indicator text are governed by the official Applied Mathematics 2202 units and indicators spreadsheet (85 unique indicators across 103 unit placements, codes MA2202.<target>.<indicator>; the app shows the number without the MA2202 prefix, following the junior apps' convention). The public curriculum viewer's indicator listings (85 unique across 103 listings) reconcile exactly with the spreadsheet. The practice-test shape comes from the Mathematics 2202 development shell's Unit 1 Test 2025, used as a structural model only, and the shell's practice catalogue informed item types and lesson topics: the shell holds only 51 H5P practice items in 43 lessons, the thinnest practice layer of the three applied courses, with ten lessons carrying none, so the bank is built deliberately fuller than the shell, especially in Units 3, 5 and 8 (Unit 5, Budgets, has a single practice item in the entire shell). No question text, scenario, or values from the shell pools, the viewer, or any other source are reproduced, and the app does not mirror the actual unit tests item for item.

Reference panels: the official Math 2202 Formula Sheet (2025-2026) holds 14 formulas, and every one is placed in the unit whose indicators use it: Unit 2 carries the five surface area formulas, Unit 3 the five volume and capacity formulas, Unit 6 the three financial formulas, and Unit 7 the slope formula. Units 1, 4, 5 and 8 carry no panel, because the sheet has no content for them (it lists no drawing, graph, budget or trigonometry formulas). Nothing on the sheet is left unplaced.

Levels: no table of specifications is published for this course. The bank's level mix (about 20% Level 1, 60% Level 2, 20% Level 3; actual whole-bank census 19.6 / 60.3 / 20.1) follows the 20/60/20 split used across the other courses as a design prior, not a specification.
